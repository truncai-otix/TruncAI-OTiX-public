What is TruncAI-OTiX?

TruncAI-OTiX V2.1 is an internally executable technical reference for bounded AI decision support, traceability and controlled technical evaluation in a local, synthetic and non-operational environment.

Current authority boundary: C0 observe, C1 analyse and C2 recommend are permitted within the tested object. C3 preparation/issuance and C4 execution/control are prohibited. Human decisions occur outside the advisory runtime.

What problem does it address?

It is designed to make advisory AI trajectories easier to govern, inspect, replay and review by combining evidence records, capability constraints, deterministic synthetic assurance and baseline-closure checks.

What are its four public components?

  • Evidence Spine — versioned, integrity-linked records supporting traceability and replay within the tested scope.
  • Agent Governance — constraints around identity, ownership, purpose, tools, privilege, validity, budgets, revocation and human escalation.
  • Assurance Lab — deterministic synthetic scenarios, negative testing and virtual-case campaigns.
  • Baseline Closure — checks intended to prevent silent expansion of authority, validation scope or claims.

What has been internally reported?

The recorded V2.1 regression reports 113/113 internal tests passing across the four components, including a deterministic campaign of 10,000 virtual synthetic cases. These results are internal, synthetic and not independently reproduced or externally validated.

What remains unresolved?

Continuity evidence and evidence of real human review remain INCONCLUSIVE and are not counted as PASS. TruncAI-OTiX is not claimed to be production-ready, externally or industrially validated, certified, regulatorily conformant by default, secure against all threats or proven in continuous operation.
