What it is

AI decision support should be governable — not just convincing.

TruncAI-OTiX V2.1 provides a local, synthetic and non-operational reference for capturing, governing, verifying and reproducing trajectories of consultative AI. The emphasis is on evidence, reproducibility, traceability, explicit limits and fail-closed behavior.

EVALUATION PROBLEM

Outputs do not explain the full decision path.

Reviewers may need more than a persuasive recommendation.

A useful evaluation can require reconstruction of the sources, applicable policy, operating limits, human intervention and resulting outcome.

DESIGN PROPOSITION

Keep authority, evidence and verification in one bounded pattern.

The baseline connects a C0–C2 ceiling with structured evidence and controlled review.

Fail-closed policy, replayable records and a progressive evaluation path are combined in one internally executable reference.

01

Evidence Spine

Versioned contracts, deterministic canonicalisation, SHA-256 records, append-only linked events, replay, manifests and integrity checks.

02

Agent Governance

Identity, human ownership, minimum-privilege delegation, purpose/tool binding, validity, budgets, revocation and HITL escalation.

03

Assurance Lab

A synthetic runtime for deterministic evaluation campaigns, negative testing, payload/finding tamper detection and replay.

04

Baseline Closure

Machine-readable closure with fail-closed validation intended to reject silent expansion of authority, validation scope or claims.

Capability boundary

Observe → Analyse → Recommend → Human decision

The current baseline deliberately caps authority at C2. It is designed so that consultative AI does not become an operational action path.

Controlled evaluation

A technical asset that can be examined — not a promise that must be believed.

The preferred transfer path is progressive: public understanding first, then scoped technical review, synthetic clean-room evaluation and only later — when justified and authorised — deeper confidential evidence.

Scoped review

Synthetic evaluation

Independent installation, tests, deterministic evidence generation, integrity and replay checks.

Confidential review

Additional evidence under explicit authorisation, confidentiality, access, retention and deletion terms.

Potential regulatory relevance — not conformity

The implemented mechanisms may support a future applicability and gap assessment concerning risk management, data governance, record-keeping, transparency, human oversight, robustness and cybersecurity. Applicability, classification and obligations depend on intended purpose, actor role and deployment context, and require competent external legal and sectoral assessment.

Evaluator FAQ

Questions to resolve before a controlled review.

Is TruncAI-OTiX a SaaS product?

No. It is a privately owned technical asset offered for controlled evaluation and possible integration, licence or acquisition discussions. It is not a production service.

Can the current baseline control operational assets?

No. Current authority is capped at C2 recommendation. C3 preparation or issue and C4 execution or control are prohibited.

Why is the technical source not public?

The website and approved overview are D0 public. Source, tests and evaluation artefacts are D2 controlled; private data, credentials and operational details remain excluded.

What can an approved evaluator inspect?

The agreed scope may include the source perimeter, installation guide, contracts and schemas, test suites, manifests and checksums, SBOM/AI-BOM, synthetic demonstration and evidence packs, and the limitations register.

Does the project claim EU AI Act conformity?

No. The mechanisms may be relevant to a future applicability and gap assessment, but no regulatory conformity or certification is claimed.

What remains unverified?

Independent cold execution, external review, portability with evaluator data, real human oversight, industrial continuity and sector-specific validation remain pending or inconclusive.
