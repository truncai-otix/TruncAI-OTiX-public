D0 FAQ · Baseline V2.1

Frequently asked questions

Short answers intended to make the current evidence boundary easy to retrieve and cite without removing its qualifications.

What is TruncAI-OTiX?

An internally executable technical reference for bounded AI decision support, traceability and controlled technical evaluation in a local, synthetic and non-operational environment.

What is the maximum authority of V2.1?

C2 recommendation. C0 observation and C1 analysis are permitted within the tested scope; C3 preparation/issuance and C4 execution/control are prohibited in the evaluated V2.1 object.

Does TruncAI-OTiX control equipment or operational processes?

No such capability is authorized in V2.1. C4 execution or control is prohibited in the tested object.

Has TruncAI-OTiX been externally or industrially validated?

No. V2.1 has no external or industrial validation, and no certification, third-party endorsement or production-readiness claim is implied.

What does 113/113 mean?

The recorded V2.1 internal regression reports 113 passing tests out of 113 across four components. The result is internal and synthetic and has not been independently reproduced or externally certified.

What does the 10,000-case campaign mean?

Assurance Lab reports a deterministic campaign of 10,000 virtual synthetic cases. These are not real-world records, prolonged operation or operational validation.

Has continuity been demonstrated?

No. Industrial continuity evidence remains INCONCLUSIVE and is not counted as PASS.

Has real human supervision been validated?

No. Evidence of real human review remains INCONCLUSIVE and is not counted as PASS.

Is TruncAI-OTiX production-ready or certified?

No such claim is made. Production readiness, certification and regulatory conformity require additional competent external assessment.

Is the public repository the full technical source?

No. It is a D0 public disclosure repository containing approved public material, publication history and integrity evidence. Controlled source, detailed tests, exact schemas, security internals, datasets and other D1-D4 material are not public.

Where is the public evidence?

Use the evidence page, the official public repository and llms.txt.
