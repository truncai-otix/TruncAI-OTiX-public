What evidence can a public reviewer verify?

The D0 surface deliberately separates public publication evidence from internally reported technical results.

Evidence classes

ClassMeaning
Publicly verifiableA reviewer can inspect the cited public artifact directly.
Internally evidencedThe result is recorded in the controlled V2.1 evidence set, but the executable material required for independent reproduction is not public at D0.
InconclusiveThe available evidence does not support a PASS claim.
Not claimedThe project expressly does not assert the outcome.

Publicly verifiable today

  • The canonical website and its public positioning, capability boundary and limitations.
  • The official public repository, history and D0 publication log.
  • The public claims matrix, high-level architecture and machine-readable manifest.
  • The SHA-256 checksums published for the four D0 capsule artifacts.
  • The machine-readable llms.txt overview.

What D0 does not prove

D0 does not independently demonstrate runtime execution, security, regulatory conformity, industrial suitability, production readiness, continuous availability, effective human supervision, operational performance or savings.
