D0 evaluation overview · Baseline V2.1

How can TruncAI-OTiX be evaluated?

The intended path moves from public understanding to progressively more controlled technical review. Public D0 material is for discovery and initial diligence, not independent reproduction of the controlled runtime.

1 · Public overview

Inspect the website, D0 repository, claims matrix, architecture, manifest, publication history and integrity hashes.

2 · Scoped technical review

Define the review purpose, evidence needs, confidentiality boundary and authority limits for a qualified third party.

3 · Synthetic clean-room evaluation

Evaluate controlled technical material and synthetic scenarios under explicit authorization without treating D0 as a source-code release.

4 · Confidential review

Where justified, deeper controlled material may be reviewed under an appropriate authorization and confidentiality framework.

5 · Third-party environment

Only when justified and authorized, a third party may evaluate transfer to its own context. Portability to a real third-party environment has not yet been demonstrated.

What has been internally verified?

The recorded V2.1 regression reports 113/113 internal tests passing across Evidence Spine, Agent Governance, Assurance Lab and Baseline Closure. Assurance Lab also reports a deterministic campaign of 10,000 virtual synthetic cases.

Qualification: these results are internal and synthetic. They are not independently reproduced, externally certified or equivalent to industrial validation or operating history.

What remains inconclusive?

  • Industrial continuity evidence — INCONCLUSIVE.
  • Evidence of real human review — INCONCLUSIVE.

Is there applied domain evidence?

Energy is the only current domain with project-specific applied evidence. The frozen V2.0 Energy Advisory demonstrator is local, read-only and consultative. It analyses historical telemetry and does not control inverters, batteries, protections, loads or the grid. Railway and other sector applications remain transfer hypotheses and have not been validated.

What is not claimed?

No external or industrial validation, certification, regulatory conformity, production readiness, functional safety, absolute security, quantified operational benefit or endorsement by an external organization is claimed.
