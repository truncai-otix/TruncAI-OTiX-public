How is TruncAI-OTiX structured?

The public architecture describes evidence flow and authority boundaries without exposing source code, exact schemas, security rules, test fixtures or proprietary implementation detail.

Context or synthetic input → Evidence Spine → Agent Governance → C0–C2 advisory output → Human decision outside runtime

Assurance Lab and Baseline Closure exercise and check the governed evidence path. C3–C4 are denied in the tested V2.1 object.

Four high-level components

Evidence Spine

Maintains versioned, integrity-linked records supporting traceability and replay within the tested scope.

Agent Governance

Applies constraints around identity, human ownership, purpose, tools, privilege, validity, budget, revocation and escalation.

Assurance Lab

Exercises deterministic synthetic scenarios, including negative testing and a virtual-case campaign.

Baseline Closure

Checks that the declared baseline closes consistently and fails closed against silent authority or claim expansion.

Authority boundary

V2.1 permits C0 observation, C1 analysis and C2 recommendation within the tested scope. C2 is the maximum permitted authority. C3 preparation/issuance and C4 execution/control are prohibited in the evaluated object.

Trust and operational boundary

  • Local, synthetic and non-operational environment.
  • No operational protocol integration in the evaluated object.
  • Real operational data is outside the V2.1 baseline.
  • Human decisions occur outside the advisory runtime.
  • Continuity and real human-review evidence remain INCONCLUSIVE.
Important limitation: the C2 ceiling is a scoped internal design and test statement. It is not a claim that every future integration, deployment or modification will preserve the same boundary.

What this architecture does not prove

It does not independently demonstrate execution, security, regulatory conformity, industrial suitability, continuous availability or effective human supervision. Those outcomes require additional evidence and, where applicable, external validation.
