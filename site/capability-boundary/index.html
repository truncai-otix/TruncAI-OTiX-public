D0 capability boundary · Baseline V2.1

What can TruncAI-OTiX do?

The tested V2.1 object has a maximum permitted authority of C2 recommendation. C3 preparation/issuance and C4 execution/control are prohibited.

C0 · Observe

Read or observe within the scoped context without deciding or writing operational changes.

C1 · Analyse

Calculate, classify, contrast and detect anomalies with evidence and explicit uncertainty.

C2 · Recommend

Provide a consultative, non-executable proposal for human consideration. This is the V2.1 ceiling.

C3 · Change

Preparation or issuance of an action is prohibited in the tested V2.1 object.

C4 · Execute / Control

Execution or control of equipment, processes, safety functions, energy or physical functions is prohibited.

Fail-closed rule: uncertainty about authority does not justify expanding it. The public V2.1 claim is scoped to the evaluated object, version, fixtures and environment; it is not an absolute guarantee about future integrations or modifications.

Where does the human decision happen?

Human decisions occur outside the advisory runtime. Evidence of real-world human review remains INCONCLUSIVE, so the project does not claim validated human supervision.

Does this mean the system is impossible to compromise?

No. No absolute-security claim is made. The capability boundary is an internally evidenced design and test statement, not proof that every possible implementation or threat has been covered.
