D0 capability boundary · Baseline V2.1

What can TruncAI-OTiX do?

The tested V2.1 object has a maximum permitted authority of C2 recommendation. C3 preparation/issuance and C4 execution/control are prohibited.

C0 · Observe

Read or observe within the scoped context without deciding or writing operational changes.

C1 · Analyse

Calculate, classify, contrast and detect anomalies with evidence and explicit uncertainty.

C2 · Recommend

Provide a consultative, non-executable proposal for human consideration. This is the V2.1 ceiling.

C3 · Change

Preparation or issuance of an action is prohibited in the tested V2.1 object.

C4 · Execute / Control

Execution or control of equipment, processes, safety functions, energy or physical functions is prohibited.

Fail-closed rule: uncertainty about authority does not justify expanding it. The public V2.1 claim is scoped to the evaluated object, version, fixtures and environment; it is not an absolute guarantee about future integrations or modifications.

How should the C0–C4 scale be interpreted?

The scale describes authority, not maturity or quality. Moving from C0 to C2 changes what the advisory object may do within the tested scope: observe, analyse, and then formulate a recommendation for human consideration. A C2 recommendation remains non-executable. It does not itself prepare, issue or apply an operational change, and it does not move the system into C3 or C4.

Unknown, ambiguous or conflicting authority is handled fail-closed rather than promoted. The boundary is evaluated together with constraints such as identity, ownership, purpose, tools, privilege, validity, budgets, revocation and escalation. Attempts above the ceiling may be retained as denied negative evidence within the tested object, but that does not prove that every future integration or modification will preserve the same behaviour.

Where does the human decision happen?

Human decisions occur outside the advisory runtime. Evidence of real-world human review remains INCONCLUSIVE, so the project does not claim validated human supervision.

How does this relate to evidence?

The capability claim should be read together with the evidence page and the declared limitations. Internal tests can support the stated C0–C2 boundary for the defined V2.1 object, but they are not a substitute for independent reproduction, sector-specific validation or evidence from an operational deployment. C3 and C4 therefore remain prohibited in the tested object, and no broader authority is inferred from the public material.

Does this mean the system is impossible to compromise?

No. No absolute-security claim is made. The capability boundary is an internally evidenced design and test statement, not proof that every possible implementation or threat has been covered.
