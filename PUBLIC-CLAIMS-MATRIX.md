## Wording that must not be used

The following claims are unsupported or exceed the V2.1 evidence boundary:

- “There is no possible route to C3 or C4.”
- “The system is impossible to compromise” or “secure against all threats.”
- “Externally validated,” “industrially validated” or “independently audited.”
- “Human supervision has been validated.”
- “Continuity has been demonstrated.”
- “Real multichannel operation has been proven.”
- “10,000 cases prove prolonged operation.”
- “Production-ready,” “certified” or “EU AI Act compliant.”
- “Patentable,” “free of third-party rights” or “freedom to operate is guaranteed.”
- Any quantified savings, accuracy, safety or operational-performance claim not backed by new approved evidence.

## Use rule

Shortening a claim must not remove its scope or qualification. New public claims require evidence review and owner approval before publication.
