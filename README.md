# TruncAI-OTiX

**Governed AI decision support with traceability, evidence and explicit human authority.**

Official website: https://truncaiotix.com

## Project status

TruncAI-OTiX V2.1 is an internally executable technical baseline designed for governable human-in-the-loop AI decision support.

The current reference is:

- local;
- synthetic;
- non-operational;
- consultative;
- bounded to C2 recommendation.

It is an internal transferable baseline with limitations and remains pending external, legal and sector-specific evaluation.

## Capability boundary

Current authorised trajectory:

**Observe → Analyse → Recommend → Human decision**

- **C0 — Observe:** scoped read-only access.
- **C1 — Analyse:** calculation, classification and evidence-bounded analysis.
- **C2 — Recommend:** non-executable recommendation for human consideration.
- **C3 — Prepare / Issue:** prohibited.
- **C4 — Execute / Control:** prohibited.

The current baseline does not authorise operational commands or equipment control.

## Internal verification

The recorded V2.1 baseline includes:

- 8 versioned evidence contracts;
- a deterministic campaign of 10,000 virtual synthetic cases;
- 113 / 113 accumulated internal tests across G7.1–G7.4.

These results apply only to the recorded object, fixtures, version and environment.

**E08 — industrial continuity:** INCONCLUSIVE.  
**E10 — real human supervision:** INCONCLUSIVE.

INCONCLUSIVE is not treated as PASS.

## Applied evidence

Energy is the only current domain with project-specific applied evidence.

The frozen V2.0 Energy Advisory demonstrator is local, read-only and consultative. It can analyse historical telemetry and produce advisory signals for human review.

It does not control inverters, batteries, protections, loads or the grid.

Railway and other sector applications remain transfer hypotheses and have not been validated.

## Public disclosure boundary

This repository is limited to approved **D0 public material**.

Not published here:

- Core source;
- private tests or Evidence Spine records;
- real energy, personal or installation data;
- credentials or endpoints;
- operational-control details;
- detailed IP or transaction records;
- D2–D4 controlled material.

Technical evaluation artefacts may be made available only through a scoped and explicitly authorised review process.

## Claim limitations

This project does **not** claim:

- external or industrial validation;
- regulatory conformity or certification;
- production readiness;
- functional safety;
- absolute security;
- proven energy, cost or emissions savings;
- forecasting or anomaly-detection accuracy;
- autonomous operational control;
- validated applicability to railway or other safety-critical sectors;
- freedom-to-operate or patentability;
- endorsement or approval by any external organisation.

## Evaluation and transfer

The intended progression is:

**Public understanding → scoped technical review → synthetic evaluation → authorised confidential review**

A qualified third party may evaluate the defined asset for possible integration, licensing or acquisition. External validation, integration, operation, safety, regulatory assessment and sector-specific validation remain outside the current internal baseline.
